Lowercase: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who we are: Austin Insurance Group LLC, the developer of Lowercase ("we", "the app").

Contact: lowercaselauncher@gmail.com

The short version

Lowercase has no accounts and no sign-up. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, messages, photos or browsing. What you type on the keyboard stays on your phone. The only things that leave your phone are a rounded location for the temperature, and basic ad-measurement events described below.

What the app does with your data

Your app list, theme and note. Stored on your phone, in the app's own storage (including a shared container between the app, its widgets and its keyboard). We never receive it.

Location (optional). If you turn on the temperature, the app asks for your location while you use it. It rounds it to about one kilometre and sends it, with a standard internet request, to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the current temperature. Open-Meteo, like any web service, sees your IP address. We don't store your location. Turn it off any time in Settings, or in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services.

The keyboard. It works without "Allow Full Access". If you turn Full Access on, the keyboard can follow your launcher theme and give haptic feedback. It does not send what you type anywhere, and it has no network code. Your keyboard also keeps a few things on your phone to work better: how you tend to hit each key, and words you've told it not to correct. Spell checking and corrections use Apple's on-device dictionary. We can't see any of it.

Photos. If you tap "Save wallpaper", the app asks permission to add one image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We receive no payment details. The app checks with Apple whether you've bought an unlock, and keeps the date your free trial started in your device's Keychain.

Ad measurement. We advertise the app on Facebook and Instagram, and we use Meta's SDK in the app to tell Meta when someone installs the app, starts the trial, or buys. These events don't include your name, contacts, location, what you type, or which apps you use. Meta may receive standard device and advertising information with them, as described in Meta's own privacy policy. We don't ask to track you across other companies' apps.

Crash and analytics. We don't run our own analytics or crash reporting. Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics if you've agreed to share them with developers in iOS settings.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't show ads inside the app. We don't read your messages, calls or notifications, and iOS doesn't let us.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can delete the app at any time, which removes everything the app stored on your phone. You can turn off location, the keyboard's Full Access, and haptics in iOS Settings. To ask a question about your data, write to lowercaselauncher@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, say so in the app.
